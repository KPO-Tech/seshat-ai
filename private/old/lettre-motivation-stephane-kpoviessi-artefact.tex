\textbf{Subject: Application for the Data Scientist -- GenAI position}

\vspace{0.4cm}

Dear Artefact Belgium Recruitment Team,

I am applying for the \textbf{Data Scientist -- GenAI} position because it closely matches the direction I have been taking over the past few years: from \textbf{Data Science and Machine Learning} towards building AI systems that can actually be integrated into real workflows.

What particularly caught my attention in this role was the emphasis on \textbf{agentic AI, tool calling and MCP}. These are not simply technologies I have been reading about; they are problems I have been actively exploring while building \textbf{Seshat}, my own agentic AI platform. Working on it has led me to think beyond the LLM itself: how agents access tools, retrieve knowledge, coordinate tasks, manage permissions, involve humans when necessary, and ultimately become reliable enough to support real business processes.

My experience at \textbf{Allianz France} gave me another perspective that I consider equally important. Working on the migration of an insurance datamart to \textbf{Python/PySpark} taught me that a technically interesting solution is not enough: \textbf{data quality, testing, non-regression and reliability} are essential when systems are expected to operate in a real organisation. This production mindset is something I now bring to the AI systems I build.

This is also why \textbf{Artefact} appeals to me. I am looking for an environment where Data Science, AI and engineering meet real business problems, and where I can learn from people who have already taken GenAI systems from experimentation to production. I would especially like to deepen my experience in areas such as \textbf{LLM evaluation, fine-tuning and large-scale GenAI deployment}, while contributing the hands-on experience I already have with \textbf{RAG, multi-agent systems and MCP}.

I recently graduated as a \textbf{Big Data \& Data Science Engineer} and I see this position as a strong opportunity to build the next stage of my career around \textbf{Generative and Agentic AI}. I am currently based in the Paris area and am open to relocating to Brussels.

I would be glad to discuss my work, including Seshat, and learn more about the GenAI projects being developed by Artefact Belgium.
