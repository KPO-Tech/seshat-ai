# Policies (Policy Bindings)

`/orgs/:id/team/policies` — Organization → Team & Access → Policies

## What it's for

The actual mechanism that grants access. A policy binding attaches a set
of permissions to a *subject* — a specific user, an entire
[team](../team-and-access/teams.mdx), or a [role](./roles.mdx) — optionally
narrowed to one specific resource (a single knowledge corpus or a single
AI provider setting) instead of applying organization-wide.

## How this relates to Roles

Assigning someone a [Role](./roles.mdx) (via
[Memberships](./memberships.mdx)) is really just a convenient way to
grant that role's whole permission bundle at once. A policy binding can
do the same thing more narrowly — for example, "give this one team read
access to this one corpus" — without touching anyone's role at all.
Roles are the coarse, everyday tool; policy bindings are for anything
that needs to be scoped tighter than "organization-wide."

## How to use it

"New binding" needs:

- A **name**, and an optional **description** (capped at 200 characters,
  shown directly under the name in the table).
- A **subject type** (User, Team, or Role) and the specific subject.
- The **permissions** to grant.
- An optional **scope** — leave it as "Organization-wide" unless you're
  restricting this grant to one specific corpus or provider setting.
