# Desktop Policies

`/orgs/:id/automation/desktop-policies` — Organization → Automation → Desktop Policies

## What it's for

Restricting what a connected desktop app is allowed to do locally —
custom AI providers, local models, multiple workspaces, changing its own
settings. Every policy is **allowed by default**; a binding here can only
restrict further, never loosen it back up.

## How to use it

The "Policy catalog" card is read-only reference — every policy this
version of the app understands, and what each one controls. Bindings are
what actually take effect: "New binding" picks a policy, who it applies
to (whole organization, a role, a specific user, or a team), and whether
it's Allowed or Blocked.

When more than one binding could apply to the same device, **the most
restrictive one wins** — a Blocked binding anywhere in the chain stays
blocked, regardless of a more permissive one elsewhere.
