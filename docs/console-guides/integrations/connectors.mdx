# Connectors

`/orgs/:id/connectors` — Organization → Integrations → Connectors

## What it's for

Two distinct pieces that share a page because they're both "external
system access," not because they're the same thing:

- **OAuth apps** — register this organization's own app credentials,
  once per provider, for **any** of the 20 OAuth connector kinds (Google
  Drive, Slack, Notion, Jira, Salesforce, ...). One registration serves
  every account this org connects afterward for that kind, whether it's
  used for knowledge_sync right below on this page, or a self-service
  agent-tool connection from Workspace → Connections.
- **Connected sources** — the actual synced accounts, each pulling
  documents into one specific [Knowledge](../ai-core/knowledge.mdx)
  corpus. This half is knowledge_sync-only: only 4 of the 20 OAuth kinds
  (Google Drive, SharePoint, OneDrive, Confluence) support it at all -
  the other 16 are agent-action tools, connected per-employee from
  Workspace → Connections instead, never listed here.

## How to use it

**OAuth apps**: pick a kind's card, "Configure" opens a Client ID/secret
form. Zendesk is the one kind that also needs its account subdomain
(its OAuth endpoints are hosted per-account, e.g. `acme.zendesk.com` —
every other provider here uses one fixed endpoint). The 8 static-secret
kinds (Stripe, Telegram, Trello, ...) never appear here at all - they
have no app to register; the connecting employee pastes their own
secret directly.

**Connected sources**: needs a corpus to sync into - **create one under
Knowledge → Corpora first, or "New connector" stays disabled** (hovering
it explains why, but it's easy to miss). S3-compatible/Azure Blob take static credentials entered
directly; the 4 OAuth kinds need that kind's app registered above first,
then "Connect with..." opens the real consent flow in a new tab. Per
source: "Sync now" queues an immediate sync outside its normal interval,
the history icon shows every past attempt including failures, and
disconnecting only removes the connection - already-ingested files stay
in the corpus.
